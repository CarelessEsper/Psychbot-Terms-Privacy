# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and sharing of personal information by Psychbot ("the Bot"), a Discord bot developed by Careless Esper (the "Bot Owner"). By using Psychbot, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

Psychbot collects certain required information that you provide to us through the use of Psychbot and the Discord servers it is part of. This information includes your Discord user ID and username, server and channel information, and limited message content. 

All collected information is subject to this Privacy Policy and that of Discord under the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to as part of Psychbot's moderation features, including to issue warnings, raise reports to server moderators, provide support and respond to your requests. We may also use your information to comply with legal obligations.

Your personal data is stored on our locally-hosted server and will not be sent to any third-party. Psychbot does not utilize AI in its services and will never sell or train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model collected as part of its operation.

Psychbot does not have access to any individual’s real name, address, financial information or similar, unless they have chosen to use it in their Discord username or display name or have otherwise provided it directly.

## Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. However, we may disclose your information in response to legal process or if required under an order from a law enforcement agency or regulatory authority.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide Psychbot’s services or as required by law. We will delete your information upon your request or when it is no longer needed.

If Psychbot is removed from a Discord server, it will retain the database for that server only for a period of 30 days unless a data deletion request is received. 

Everyone has the right to know the details and extent of their data collected by Psychbot, or to have that data expunged. This right supersedes all of the above. To request your data or to request that it be expunged, please contact the Bot Owner at psychbotsupport@gmail.com. Please note that since your email address is not collected by our services, you will need to provide sufficient evidence that the person making the request is the same as the person whose data is being requested.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no security measure is perfect, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, and we will post the updated policy on this page. Your continued use of Psychbot after we make changes to this policy indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at psychbotsupport@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy is effective as of August 18, 2026.
